# Collected data

Redistributable public datasets and detection artifacts are stored under `data/raw/` and may be tracked in Git when their licenses are preserved. Their provenance, retrieval metadata, licenses, and SHA-256 hashes belong in the research manifests before the artifacts are used.

Publisher-copyright vendor reports and government PDFs remain local and are excluded from Git. The source registry records their URLs and hashes without redistributing them.

Do not place malware binaries in this directory.
